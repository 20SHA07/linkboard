# Linkboard

Your links, your space. Create a personal page with your social profiles, projects, and everything you want to share.

[Open Linkboard](https://20sha07.github.io/linkboard/)

- Customize colours, backgrounds, typography, layouts, and individual links.
- Switch between light and dark modes.
- Preview your page on desktop and mobile.
- Add an on-page QR code and download SVG or PNG versions.
- Export your website and keep an editable backup.

The interactive demo saves changes in your browser. Sign-in and public publishing are unavailable in the demo. Download a backup to keep your work.
