A COUPLE OF GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you make yourself at home.

Is Linkboard free to use?

Yes. There’s no paid tier in this app and no charge for design tools, QR codes, or exports. This site has capacity limits to keep it running. You can see your page allowance in your account.

Can I use it for a club or community?

Absolutely. Make a page for QMC, a society, a project, or yourself. Each page has its own address, style, and set of links. One account owns each page.

Can I take my page with me?

Download an editable backup or export a standalone website from Settings. The website export can be hosted on GitHub Pages or another static host.

What happens when I change a link?

Your published page address stays the same. Save your updates and existing QR codes will still lead to the latest version.
