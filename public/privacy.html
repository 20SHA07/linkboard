Your information on Linkboard

This page describes how this Linkboard site handles information.

Your account

Supabase Auth handles your email, password, verification, and password recovery. The site stores your account ID, email, display name, and encrypted session credentials in its Cloudflare D1 database. Your password is sent securely to Supabase and is not saved in Linkboard’s database.

Your pages

Page names, links, uploaded images, and design settings are stored in the site database. Drafts can be accessed through your account. Published pages can be viewed by anyone, and search engines may index them. Unpublishing stops new access on this site, but cannot erase copies others already saved.

Cookies and local preferences

A necessary sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Your colour-mode preference is saved in your browser. There are no advertising cookies in this app. Optional security checks are provided by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Page statistics

Page owners can switch on daily view and click totals. These counts do not create visitor profiles. Requests with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals are excluded. Hosting and account providers may separately keep security logs under their own policies.

Security and reports

Short-lived hashed network identifiers are used to limit repeated account requests and page reports. A report stores its text, page reference, and submission time for the site owner to review. Avoid adding personal information to a report.

Exporting or removing information

You can export, unpublish, and delete your pages in Settings. Deleted pages may remain temporarily in hosting backups. For account deletion, report removal, or other information requests, contact the operator of this Linkboard site. The operator must also remove your Supabase Auth record when deleting your account.

See Supabase’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their handling of service data.
