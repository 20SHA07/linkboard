LINKBOARD / COMMUNITY

Community terms

Linkboard gives you a place to share your links. Please use it in ways that respect the people who visit.

What you share

You keep ownership of your content. By uploading or publishing it, you allow this site to store and display it so your page works. Only share material you own or have permission to use.

Keep the space useful

Do not publish phishing, malware, scams, unlawful material, harassment, impersonation, or content that violates another person’s rights. Do not use automation to overwhelm the service or create accounts to evade limits.

Your account and pages

Keep your password private. You are responsible for your account and the links you publish. A page’s public address is fixed after its first publication so shared links and QR codes continue working. Deleted addresses may become available again, so unpublish a page if you want to keep its address.

Availability and limits

This app has no subscription or paid feature tier. Hosting capacity is limited, and the operator may pause registrations, change usage limits, or take the service offline. Keep backups of content you want to retain. External websites linked from pages have their own rules and availability.

Reports and moderation

The site owner may hide pages or disable accounts that misuse the service. Use Report a page to flag an issue. If you believe a page was hidden by mistake, contact the operator of this Linkboard site.
